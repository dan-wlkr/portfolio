About me

I’m a maker at heart. I’ve got a 3D printer that gets plenty of use, and I love designing things, tinkering and working out how stuff fits together.

Most of my favourite projects start as a quick sketch and end up as something I can actually pick up and use. Art school got me thinking about what happens when you pull things apart and put them back together again, and I’ve never really stopped.

Most weekends you’ll find me on a cargo bike with a couple of kids on the back, or doing my best Unai Emery impression on the sideline, managing a kids’ football team.

Outside of that, I love music, art and going to gigs. I’m always reading too, usually fiction and very slowly (I’m very happy if I hit 12 books a year).
