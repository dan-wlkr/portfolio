Vaccine catch-up

Missing vaccines — POC

Prototype for a mobile app to find which vaccines a child is missing when their history is incomplete, for example after arriving in a new country.

Flow

  • Child — date of birth and what kind of record exists.
  • Records — per dose: given, not given or unsure. Only doses due for the child's age are asked about.
  • Result — what's missing, a catch-up plan split into visits, what's coming up, and what's no longer needed.

Rules in this POC

  • Unsure, or reported without a written record, counts as not given (standard practice when status is uncertain).
  • Some vaccines have age limits, such as rotavirus and MenB, so they drop out instead of being caught up.
  • Catch-up visits are 4 weeks apart, and each dose respects the minimum interval since the previous one.

Caveat

The schedule is based on the UK routine childhood programme and simplified for demo purposes. Not clinical advice.
